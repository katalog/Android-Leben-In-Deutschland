BRIEF310 — Leben in Deutschland
Assumption

Mixed audience — A1 arrivals through B1 citizenship applicants. The catalogue is fixed: 300 general questions plus 10 per Bundesland, so the app is named for it. The exam simulation is accurate: 33 questions, 60 minutes, 17 correct to pass.

Language model

German is always the primary text — the real exam is German-only, so the app must never let a learner read only their own language. Translation is a second layer: revealed, side-by-side, or full RTL. Interface labels stay German with the translation beneath.

Why this style fits

Modernist's flat 2px rules, zero radius and single red read as official rather than playful — the register of the document this test belongs to. It also buys clarity: rules separate answers without boxes, and large flush-left Archivo survives being read in a second language.

Open questions

Audio read-aloud? Offline download? Is the Bundesland pack free or paid? And should wrong answers feed a spaced-repetition queue — that would change the home screen materially.
